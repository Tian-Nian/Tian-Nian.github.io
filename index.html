Detailed Profile (详细个人介绍)

Hi! I am Tian Nian (Chinese name: 粘天), an Algorithm Engineer at Xspark AI (无界智航) in Shenzhen. I received my B.Eng. in Software Engineering (Tencent Class) from Shenzhen University in 2025, and was a research intern at Shanghai AI Laboratory from Mar. 2025 to Sep. 2025. My work focuses on real-robot Embodied AI: building the systems that connect robot hardware, teleoperation, data pipelines, and learned policies, so that research models can actually run on physical robots.

I created control_your_robot, an open-source pipeline covering robotic arm control, data collection, and VLA training and deployment. I am a core lead author of XPolicyLab, which received the Best Tool Paper Award at the IROS 2026 ScaleInfra Workshop, and a contributor to RoboTwin 2.0 (ICML 2026).
